📖 How to Play / 遊戲說明

1. Spot the Day (✨)

Spin the spotlight to pick the target day.

尋找幸運日 (✨)： 轉動聚光燈選出目標日期。

2. Roll the Dice (🎲)

Pick both students at once or click each badge/button to pick individually.

擲骰子 (🎲)： 一次抽選兩位或分別點擊按鈕/號碼牌個別抽選。

3. Check Your Number (🔍)

See if your number is picked.

檢查號碼 (🔍)： 確認是否為你的號碼。

4. Speak & Score! (🗣️)

Stand up, ask or answer from the boxes, and get your points!

開口說並得分！ (🗣️)： 請站起來進行問答並獲取分數！
